SOLMAR BAY

Streets of Gold — an open-world crime story

Rico Vega came to Solmar Bay to drive. Mara Solis has bigger plans: six jobs, one crew, and a city run by Dante Kross and his Vultures. Steal cars, outrun the police, take the plaza, rob the bank — and take the city.

W A S D — move / drive  ·  Mouse drag — look around
Shift — run  ·  Space — jump / handbrake
E — steal / enter a car, get out  ·  Click — shoot  ·  F — punch
R — reload  ·  P — pause  ·  M — mute
Follow the yellow marker. Crime raises your wanted level — lose the police to cool off. Progress saves automatically after every mission.

An original game. Solmar Bay, Rico Vega, Mara Solis and the Vultures are fictional — no characters, places or art from any commercial game.
